Ditch the guesswork when starting your next hat project! Whether you're working in inches or centimeters, using baby yarn or super bulky wool.

### How to Use This Beanie Calculator
Working out stitch math shouldn't feel like a geometry test! This tool takes your personal measurements and your yarn's unique gauge to figure out exactly how many stitches to cast on and how many rows to knit. 

* **Head Circumference:** Measure around the widest part of the head (usually just above the ears and across the forehead). 

* **Gauge (Stitches and Rows):** This is just a measure of how tightly or loosely you knit. Knit a small square swatch (about 10 cm by 10 cm), count how many stitches fit across a unit of measurement, and do the same for the rows going up. Because yarn and needle sizes change everything, this ensures your beanie actually fits!

* **Negative Ease:** Don't worry about subtracting centimeters for a snug fit—the calculator automatically applies a standard 10% reduction so your finished beanie hugs the head nicely instead of sliding off.
