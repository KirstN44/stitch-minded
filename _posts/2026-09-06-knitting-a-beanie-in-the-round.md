Knitting a beanie in the round using circular needles is one of the most rewarding fiber craft projects. It eliminates side seams, keeps tension consistent, and makes sizing straightforward to adjust.

### Essential Gear Setup
* **4mm (US 6) Circular Needles:** A 40cm (16") cable length is ideal for adult beanie circumferences.
* **Yarn:** Standard DK or Worsted weight yarn.
* **Stitch Markers:** One distinct marker to identify the start of the round.
* **Tapestry Needle:** For weaving in ends and closing the crown.

### Key Steps for Success

1. **Casting On:** Cast on your required stitch count loosely to ensure the brim retains comfortable stretch.
2. **Joining in the Round:** Double-check that your cast-on stitches are not twisted around the cable before working your first stitch. Place your main stitch marker here.
3. **Working the Brim:** Knit in a 1x1 or 2x2 ribbing pattern for 4–5 cm (1.5–2") to create a snug, elastic edge.
4. **The Body & Crown:** Transition to stockinette stitch (knitting every round) until reaching your desired crown height—typically 18–20 cm (7–8") total height from the cast-on edge before beginning decreases. Work structured decreases using double-pointed needles or the magic loop technique as the stitch count shrinks.

### Quick Tip for Clean Edges
When joining your first round, slip the first stitch from the left needle to the right needle and pass the last cast-on stitch over it. This bridges the join tightly and prevents an unsightly gap at the brim.
